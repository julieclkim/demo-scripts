Payment call flow

A concise path agents can follow while the embedded script is open.

Step Agent action Source field
1 Confirm identity using student ID and contact details. 123456, (555) 010-1010, student@example.edu
2 State the outstanding balance and whether a hold is active. $0, No
3 Review payment plan eligibility and available options. Yes, Monthly installment plan, one-time payment, employer reimbursement documentation
4 Direct the student to the payment portal and document the next action. Review account and document the outcome.
